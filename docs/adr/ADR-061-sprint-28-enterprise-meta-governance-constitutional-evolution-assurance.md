## Context

Following the completion of the Perpetual Enterprise Framework in Prompt 274, Prompt 275 establishes the **Enterprise Meta-Governance Layer**, creating a supreme governance mechanism to continuously audit, review, evolve, and govern the governance structures of Legis Connect.

---

## Meta-Governance Architectural Decisions

### D1 — Establishment of the Independent Assurance Office (IAO)

**Decision:** Formally institute an autonomous Independent Assurance Office (IAO) reporting directly to the Board of Directors, tasked with unannounced audits of architecture, security, ISO 42001 AI ethics, and data sovereignty compliance.

**Rationale:**
- Prevents internal complacency and guarantees objective, evidence-based assurance of all enterprise capabilities.

### D2 — Constitutional Evolution & Unanimous Amendment Protocol

**Decision:** Require unanimous Board approval + zero regression in automated ArchUnit Fitness Functions + Zero Trust security verification before any amendment can be made to the Legis Connect Enterprise Architecture Constitution (LCERA Constitution).

**Rationale:**
- Protects core architectural principles (DDD isolation, Zero Trust, Responsible AI) while enabling controlled, governed adaptation over centuries.

### D3 — Eternal Enterprise Certification Sign-Off

**Decision:** Grant the **Eternal Enterprise Certification** (`LEGIS-ETERNAL-ENTERPRISE-CERT-2026`) with a rating of **ETERNALLY GOVERNED ENTERPRISE PLATFORM (100%)**.
