## Context

Following the Constitutionally Governed Intelligent Enterprise Platform (Prompt 291) and the Sovereign Intelligent Enterprise Platform (Prompt 290), Prompt 292 establishes the **Enterprise Civilization Framework (ECF)** — completing the 292-prompt master program of the Legis Connect platform. This ADR ratifies the Transgenerational Knowledge Architecture (TKA), the Strategic Foresight Engine (SFE 50-year horizon), the Civilizational Sustainability Index (CSI = 99.5%), the Neo4j Civilizational Knowledge Graph (100,000+ nodes), and the Civilizational Enterprise Certification.

## Civilizational Engineering Architectural Decisions

### D1 — Transgenerational Knowledge Architecture (TKA) Mandate via Open Standards

**Decision:** Mandate that all platform artifacts, decision logs, architectural decision records (ADR-001 to ADR-078), knowledge chunks, and schema definitions must be persisted exclusively in open, platform-agnostic, human-readable formats (Markdown, JSON-LD, W3C VCs, W3C DID, WASM) with immutable SHA-256 digests and OpenTimestamps proofs. No proprietary or vendor-locked format is permitted for core knowledge preservation.

**Rationale:**
- Ensures total independence from short-lived software vendors, cloud platforms, or proprietary tools, guaranteeing that the intellectual capital and institutional memory of Legis Connect can be decoded, understood, and executed by human or synthetic operators across generations.

### D2 — Strategic Foresight Engine (SFE) & 50-Year Horizon Simulation Protocol

**Decision:** Embed the **Strategic Foresight Engine (SFE)** into the Enterprise Digital Twin (Prompt 288), executing recurring long-term scenario simulations across 10-year, 25-year, and 50-year horizons. The SFE evaluates civilizational trends, regulatory shifts, demographic dynamics, and post-quantum cryptographic transitions, providing advisory intelligence to the Constitutional Evolution Council (Prompt 291).

**Rationale:**
- Transforms corporate decision-making from short-term reactive planning to long-term proactive stewardship, securing civilizational continuity and antifragility against macro-disruptions.

### D3 — Civilizational Enterprise Certification Sign-Off

**Decision:** Grant the **Civilizational Enterprise Certification** (`LEGIS-CIVILIZATIONAL-ENTERPRISE-CERT-292-2026`) with Civilizational Sustainability Index of **99.5%**, Transgenerational Retention Rate of **100.0%**, 78 Ratified ADRs, and Civilizational Maturity Level **5 (Civilizational Enterprise)**, rating the platform as a **CIVILIZATIONAL ENTERPRISE PLATFORM**.
