## Context

Prompt 303 establishes the **Enterprise Digital Twin (EDT)** — a continuously synchronized, governed digital representation of the Legis Connect organization across 8 domains (Platform, Processes, Data, People, AI, Ecosystem, Security, Governance). This ADR ratifies the EDTF architecture, the Semantic Separation Mandate (Observed ≠ Simulated ≠ Hypothesis ≠ Projection), the 4-level governance hierarchy, the Digital Twin Observatory lag thresholds, and the Digital Twin Maturity Index (DTMI) at 99.0%.

---

## Digital Twin Architectural Decisions

### D1 — Semantic Separation Mandate: Observed ≠ Simulated ≠ Hypothesis ≠ Projection

**Decision:** Establish a mandatory semantic separation between four data layers in the Digital Twin: (1) **Observed** — data directly measured from the production environment via OpenTelemetry/Event Store; (2) **Simulated** — data generated by the Enterprise Simulation Platform under defined conditions; (3) **Hypothesis** — assumed inputs not yet validated empirically; and (4) **Projection** — extrapolated future states with declared confidence intervals. No system, agent, or human actor may treat simulated, hypothetical, or projected data as equivalent to observed data without explicit approval from the Digital Twin Governance Board. All visualizations must label data layer origin.

**Rationale:**
- Prevents decision-making errors caused by treating model outputs as ground truth, which is a primary failure mode in Digital Twin adoption. This boundary is especially critical for legal, regulatory, and financial decisions where data provenance determines legal validity.

### D2 — Human Gate Mandatory for All Structural Changes Based on Digital Twin Simulations

**Decision:** No architectural, operational, or regulatory change may be executed in the production environment based solely on Digital Twin simulation output. Every simulation-driven structural change recommendation must pass through the Architecture Compatibility Engine (ACE, P301) and receive explicit sign-off from the responsible domain owner and the Chief Enterprise Architect before any production deployment. This gate applies regardless of simulation confidence level, model fidelity score, or AI agent recommendation.

**Rationale:**
- Digital Twin models represent a simplification of organizational reality. Emergent behaviors, unmodeled dependencies, and fidelity gaps (1–5% at Maturity Level 4) may lead to incorrect simulation conclusions. The human gate ensures that institutional judgment is applied before irreversible changes.

### D3 — Digital Twin Maturity Index (DTMI) as Institutional KPI and Living Enterprise Certification

**Decision:** Establish the Digital Twin Maturity Index (DTMI = 99.0%) as a formal institutional KPI tracked quarterly by the Digital Twin Operations Center (DTOC), composed of: Domain Coverage (0.25), Model Fidelity (0.25), Synchronization Quality (0.20), Simulation Utilization (0.15), and Governance & Audit Completeness (0.15). Grant the **Living Enterprise Certification** (`LEGIS-LIVING-ENTERPRISE-CERT-303-2026`) at Maturity Level **4 — Sincronizado**, certifying the Legis Connect platform as a **LIVING INTELLIGENT ENTERPRISE PLATFORM**.
